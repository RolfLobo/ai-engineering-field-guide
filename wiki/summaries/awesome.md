## What it says

A curated, sectioned collection of AI engineering resources: practitioner interview stories, architecture lessons, interview question collections, system design guides, company engineering blogs, official company interview guides and AI policies, books, courses, communities, podcasts, key practitioner voices, HN/Reddit discussions, and market data.

## Sections and highlights

- AI interview experiences - first-person accounts: Mimansa Jaiswal (20+ companies), Janvi Kalra (46 organizations), Deepthi Sudharsan (50+ rounds), Fonzi AI (interviewer perspective), Mai Chi Bao (RAG take-home)
- Architecture lessons 2025-2026 - DataA.dev (7/10 GenAI pilots never reached production), context engineering, RAG blueprints, Alex Ewerlof's 30 patterns
- Interview question collections - TechEon agentic guide, DataCamp RAG/agentic sets, GitHub question hubs
- AI system design guides - Chip Huyen's GenAI platform piece, InterviewNode patterns, System Design Handbook, HelloInterview OpenAI L5
- Case studies and engineering blogs - collection entries plus per-company sections: Anthropic (Building Effective Agents, multi-agent research, contextual retrieval), Doctolib (Alfred agent, 17K daily messages), Uber (GenAI Gateway, 60+ use cases), Perplexity (200M daily queries on Vespa), Slack (stateless RAG, escrow VPC), Spotify (Honk coding agent, 1,500+ merged PRs), Shopify, DoorDash, LinkedIn, Netflix, Notion, Grab, Pinterest, Cohere, Databricks, and the cloud providers
- Company interview guides and AI candidate policies - OpenAI's official guide, Doctolib's feature-building interview, Anthropic's candidate AI guidance and open-sourced take-home, Datadog/Zapier/Oscar Health policies
- Books - Chip Huyen's AI Engineering (2025), LLM Engineer's Handbook, Generative AI System Design Interview, applied-llms.org
- Courses - From RAG to Agents (the Buildcamp the portfolio bar is grounded in), LLM Zoomcamp, Jason Liu's RAG course, Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar's evals course
- Communities, podcast interviews (DataTalks.Club episodes reused as portfolio evidence in [portfolio/01](../../portfolio/01-types-of-projects.md)), key practitioner voices (Chip Huyen, Eugene Yan, Shreya Shankar, Hamel Husain, Swyx, Karpathy), ~35 HN/Reddit discussions, market data sources (InterviewQuery, interviewing.io, Karat, Indeed Hiring Lab)
