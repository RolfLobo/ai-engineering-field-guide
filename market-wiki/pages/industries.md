# Industries

Keyword-classified industry per posting, matched in order against the company focus text, then company name, then job title. First match wins; 1,075 postings (15.4%) match no keyword and are left unclassified. This is an approximation (the dataset has no industry field) - share values are indicative, not exact. Scrape window: Feb 4, 2026 to Aug 25, 2026.
