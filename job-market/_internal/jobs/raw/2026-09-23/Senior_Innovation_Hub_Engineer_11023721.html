Summary Generated by Built In
Overview

Join Microsoft's Innovation Hub and help some of the world's largest manufacturing organisations accelerate their AI transformation. This is a highly customer-facing role that combines technical expertise, business consulting and innovation. You'll work directly with senior business and technology leaders to shape strategy, solve complex challenges and bring AI-powered solutions to life through immersive engagements and workshops. This is an on-site role based in the Microsoft Innovation Hub.


Responsibilities
  • Lead customer workshops, strategy sessions and innovation engagements focused on AI and digital transformation
  • Advise executive stakeholders on how to use AI, data and cloud technologies to achieve business outcomes
  • Design scalable, secure and high-impact solution architectures that address complex business challenges
  • Partner across sales, engineering and technical teams to drive customer success and accelerate adoption
  • Act as a trusted technical advisor, connecting innovation opportunities to measurable business value

Qualifications
  • Proven technical pre-sales, solution architecture or technical consulting experience
  • Experience engaging CIO, CTO and board-level stakeholders to shape business and technology strategy
  • Ability to design end-to-end enterprise architectures spanning cloud, AI, data, applications and security
  • Strong discovery and consulting skills, translating business objectives into technical solutions and customer outcomes
  • Manufacturing industry expertise, including Industry 4.0, smart factory and IT/OT transformation initiatives
  • Hands-on experience building prototypes, proof of concepts and solution demonstrations for enterprise customers
