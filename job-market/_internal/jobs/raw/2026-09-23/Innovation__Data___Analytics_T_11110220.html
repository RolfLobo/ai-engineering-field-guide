The Role
Designs, builds, and operates production-grade agentic AI systems and orchestration frameworks. Responsibilities include prompt architecture, tool and API integrations, monitoring, evaluation, cost controls, reliability improvements, and governance documentation. The engineer collaborates with data science, data engineering, and governance teams to ensure reliable, compliant workflows using structured healthcare and pharmaceutical data.
Summary Generated by Built In
ROLE SUMMARY
Own the orchestration layer and agent architecture behind the Innovation, Data & Analytics team's AI-based workflows, building and hardening agentic systems that translate analytical methodology into reliable production tools.
ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES
Agentic Systems Design & Build
  • Own orchestration frameworks and agent architecture for AI-based workflows
  • Build and harden agentic workflows for production use
  • Design prompt architecture and tool/API integrations across agentic systems
  • Translate the Senior Data Scientist's methodology and evaluation criteria into deployed, production-grade agents

Production Reliability & Collaboration
  • Partner with the Data Engineer to ensure agents consume clean, well-structured data
  • Partner with the Senior Data Analyst to ensure agentic workflows meet governance, audit, and compliance requirements
  • Implement monitoring, evaluation, and cost controls for LLM-based systems in production
  • Document agent architecture, prompt design, and tool integrations to support governance and audit needs
  • Proactively identify opportunities to improve workflow reliability, latency, and cost

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS
Candidate demonstrates strong technical execution and the ability to operate independently in a fast-paced, regulated environment, partnering effectively across data science, data engineering, and governance functions.
  • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Machine Learning, or a related field
  • Experience (8+ years with Bachelor's; 7+ years with Master's) in software or ML engineering, notably in the healthcare/pharma sector
  • Experience with agentic frameworks (e.g., LangGraph, Pydantic AI, or similar)
  • Strong software engineering background, including production system design and testing
  • Experience integrating LLM APIs and exposure to MLOps practices (evaluation, monitoring, cost control)
  • Healthcare data experience preferred
  • Effective English verbal and written communication with flexibility to be clear, consistent, compliant, and appropriate for a variety of settings including scientific/technical, regulatory, and business audiences

Technical:
  • Anticipates Customer and Market Needs - Knows the industry and technology landscape; is dedicated to meeting the expectations of internal and external customers; always focused on patient safety first.
  • Acts Decisively - Makes timely and effective engineering decisions; acts with urgency and removes barriers that hinder productivity.
  • Builds Change-Agile Systems - Designs agentic systems that are flexible and adaptive to evolving business needs.
  • Strategic and Innovative Thinking - Demonstrates insightful thinking by developing creative approaches to agentic system design.
  • Is a role model of Pfizer's values and behaviors.

Demonstrate ability to:
  • Drive transformational change
  • Engage diverse technical and business stakeholders
  • Understand the applications of technology to the Life Science sector
