Resilinc is hiring a Senior Digital Learning & AI Content Developer to build and scale a modern, AI-forward Academy experience for customers, partners, and internal teams.

This is a hands-on individual-contributor role for someone who can take complex product and domain knowledge and quickly turn it into clear, engaging, high-quality digital learning content.

The ideal candidate is a strong content creator, video producer, learning technologist, and AI-enabled builder. They should be comfortable researching, scripting, producing, editing, publishing, and improving digital learning assets using modern AI and multimedia tools.

Because this is expected to be a lean or solo function initially, the successful candidate must be highly self-directed and capable of owning the full content lifecycle from idea to published experience.

What You Will Do

    Create Modern Digital Learning Content

    Personally create and maintain:

    • Short-form product videos
    • Tutorials and walkthroughs
    • Customer onboarding journeys
    • Interactive learning modules
    • Product-release education
    • Role-based learning paths
    • Certifications and assessments
    • Internal enablement content
    • Quick-reference guides
    • Webinar and event content
    • AI-assisted multimedia assets

      • Turn complex technical concepts into concise, visually strong, easy-to-consume learning experiences for enterprise users.

      Use AI to Accelerate Content Production

      Use modern AI tools to improve speed and quality across:

      • Research and content planning
      • Script writing and storyboarding
      • Summarization
      • Translation and localization
      • Voiceover
      • Video editing
      • Graphics and visual creation
      • Quiz and assessment generation
      • Content repurposing
      • Knowledge extraction from product and SME sessions

        • Continuously evaluate new AI tools and workflows that can shorten production cycles and improve content quality.

        Produce High-Quality Video and Multimedia

        • Create polished product videos, tutorials, demos, and learning assets.
        • Use modern video-editing and AI-assisted production tools.
        • Apply AI-generated voice, captions, translation, animation, avatars, and visual effects where useful.
        • Build reusable templates and production standards.
        • Convert product demonstrations, SME sessions, and technical walkthroughs into scalable digital content.

          • Partner With Subject Matter Experts

          • Work directly with Product, Services, Support, Engineering, Sales, and customer-facing teams.
          • Extract knowledge quickly and efficiently from subject matter experts.
          • Convert technical discussions into clear customer-facing learning.
          • Keep content aligned with product releases, customer workflows, and strategic priorities.
          • Minimize SME time while maintaining accuracy and quality.

            • Build Scalable Learning Journeys

            • Design structured onboarding experiences for customers and internal teams.
            • Create role-based learning paths and certification programs.
            • Reduce dependence on repetitive instructor-led training.
            • Build content that helps users become self-sufficient faster.
            • Support customer adoption and product usage through digital enablement.

              • Own the Academy Content Experience

              • Organize and maintain learning content within the Academy platform.
              • Keep assets current, searchable, accessible, and easy to navigate.
              • Manage publishing, updates, versioning, and retirement of outdated content.
              • Maintain consistent quality and branding.
              • Work comfortably within an LMS while keeping the focus on content quality and user experience rather than platform administration.

                • Measure and Improve Effectiveness

                Track and improve:

                • Video and course engagement
                • Completion rates
                • Learning-path and certification progress
                • Customer and employee feedback
                • Self-service usage
                • Time to create and publish content
                • Content freshness and usage

                  • Use data to determine what content is working and what should be improved, replaced, or retired.

    • Strong collaboration with Product and customer-facing teams

What We Do

Every year, tens of thousands of events – ranging from natural disasters to factory fires to health epidemics – shut down manufacturing and wreak havoc on global supply chains. We believe a resilient supply chain is good for everyone: it keeps product flowing, the world moving, and most importantly, people in their jobs. Resilinc was founded with the purpose of strengthening global supply chains, making them resilient, sustainable, transparent, and secure. We do this via our technology-driven solutions, which create an ecosystem where organizations can collaborate with their suppliers and customers with a spirit of transparency and trust to acquire unmatched visibility into their multi-tier supply networks, and partner across tiers seamlessly to recover supply chains during disruptions. Since our launch in 2010, Resilinc has defined the supply chain mapping, monitoring, and resiliency space and is widely considered the gold standard for supply chain resiliency, worldwide. With over 1 million supplier sites mapped encompassing over 4 million parts and raw materials, we are the first line of defense for our customers, helping them navigate supply disruptions. Our early-warning alert system monitors and predicts potential disruptions across suppliers, sites, and materials; our platform enables them to collaborate closely with their suppliers; our historical data-backed insights give them options on appropriate actions to take. Always innovating, our AI-powered predictive solutions can predict delivery delays, price movements, and supply constraints for raw materials and commodities before they happen. Resilinc helps our customers protect revenue and turn supply chain risks into opportunities to gain a competitive advantage
