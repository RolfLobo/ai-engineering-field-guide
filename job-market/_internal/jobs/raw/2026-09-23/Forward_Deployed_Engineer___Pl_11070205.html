Job Description

As a Forward Deployed Engineer, Platform, you embed directly with product development teams and customer organisations to understand real problems, design real solutions, and own the outcome. You are a force multiplier for developer velocity and platform adoption, working at the sharp end, in the code, unblocking teams, and building the patterns and tooling that make platform adoption feel effortless.

This is a high-agency role for a hands-on senior engineer who thrives in ambiguity and moves fast.

  • Embed directly with product teams and customer engineering organisations to maximise developer velocity and reduce GTM timelines
  • Lead technical discovery, identify the actual bottleneck, not the presenting symptom
  • Along with a dedicated Architecture team, design and build production-grade solutions on the IFS Cloud platform: workflows, integrations, event-driven patterns, and data pipelines
  • Identify, diagnose, and resolve platform friction points preventing teams from shipping at speed
  • Champion and implement event-driven architecture patterns across product teams
  • Build tooling, patterns, and reference implementations that accelerate adoption of Kairos and Nexus, reusable assets that scale your impact beyond the teams you work with directly
  • Ship a working first version fast, then harden it: proper monitoring, iteration based on real usage, production quality
  • Work alongside the dedicated NGA architecture team to drive the requirements for the Internal Developer Platform (IDP), the core developer experience touchpoint through which product teams will consume Kairos and Nexus
  • Translate product team pain points into actionable platform feedback and roadmap input
  • Work directly with customer architects, IT leads, and business stakeholders, going deep in code while communicating clearly about trade-offs
  • Be the connective tissue between IFS R&D and the teams consuming the platform, your field insights shape what gets built next

Qualifications

  • You have 5+ years of software development experience, with strong hands-on capability across the stack:
    • Backend: Go
    • Frontend: React (with Next.js)
    • Messaging / Streaming: Apache Kafka / RedPanda
    • Data: PostgreSQL
  • You’re comfortable operating in a cloud-native environment: Kubernetes, containers, GitOps, IaC, Software Defined Infrastructure
  • You have a solid understanding of event-driven and distributed systems architecture, and you apply it pragmatically in delivery contexts
  • You’ve built and owned production systems (not just prototypes) and know how to debug things that go wrong in the real world
  • You thrive in ambiguity, you can turn a fuzzy problem into a scoped, shipped system without waiting to be asked
  • You communicate well with non-technical stakeholders without dumbing it down
  • You have experience working in or alongside platform or infrastructure teams
  • Customer-facing technical work is in your background, you know how to build trust with engineering teams outside your own
