Vectara provides an Agent Platform that allows organizations to rapidly create AI applications that are grounded in the context from their data and knowledge. Our end-to-end platform provides critical capabilities required for enterprise adoption: high accuracy even with incredibly complex data, powerful agent harness, comprehensive observability, and the ability to run in any environment (cloud, VPC, on-premise).

We build AI for doing. We are passionate about helping users take advantage of breakthroughs in applied AI to solve problems and get stuff done. Our team is a group of unquestionable all-stars in their respective fields of computer science and business. Come join us in this mission.

Role Overview:

Vectara is looking for an experienced Sales Engineer to join us. You will be connecting customers with the magic of our product, aligning our capabilities to the pain points and business opportunities they are attacking. You will remain the trusted technical point of contact throughout the customer's journey with our product.

You will work as an integral part of our Sales and Customer Success motions, and you will work hand-in-hand with our Professional Services team. This will include assisting them as they first learn about the product and conduct their evaluation, then helping as they build their first production application, then providing assistance and guidance once they have launched. By doing so you will play an integral part in delivering sales and customer satisfaction outcomes for our business.

To do this well you will maintain a strong relationship with our Product, Engineering, and Machine Learning teams, so you can effectively respond to customer needs while ensuring that what we build meets the requirements of the market. You will be the technical face of the company and build relationships with the technical leaders within the customers you serve. In this way you will positively influence your customers and help them successfully navigate their way through the AI era.

If you enjoy being a technical thought leader who provokes fresh ideas on what is possible, provides clear explanations for how to incorporate AI into impactful products, and has a very strong customer-first orientation, then we should talk.

Key Responsibilities:

While accomplishing the above big picture outcomes, your day-to-day tasks will include the following:

  • Providing technical product expertise
  • Conducting demos, including custom-built ones when needed
  • Helping with POCs
  • Assisting customers with architecture, design, and development
  • Scoping and planning PS projects
  • Conducting evaluations and performance tuning of retrieval and agentic pipelines to ensure applications meet rigorous enterprise requirements
  • Responding persuasively to functional and technical elements of RFIs/RFPs
  • Conducting regular check-in meetings with customers to update them on new features and identify opportunities for expanded usage
  • Communicating relevant feedback to internal teams to improve our product and processes

Qualifications:

  • 5+ years of Sales Engineering experience, supporting the GTM motion of companies in one of the following spaces: AI, ML, search, analytics, data engineering
  • Experience selling technical products to AI, Data, or IT organizations within large enterprises
  • 2+ years of Professional Services or internal development experience, working in large enterprises to design and develop applications
  • Experience with agentic AI development, spanning the context layer to the agent harness - skills, tools, guardrails, etc. 
  • Development experience with Python and/or Java
  • Experience developing apps that heavily consume REST services
  • Understanding of architectural principles for cloud-based platforms, including SaaS, VPC deployments, multi-tenancy, and automation
  • Understanding of architectural principles for on premise systems, including hardware, compute (especially GPUs), virtualization (especially k8s), and networking
  • Strong communication, interpersonal and collaboration skills
  • Compelling storyteller, with experience building product narratives and pitches
  • Ability to communicate complex technology concepts crisply and simply
  • Should be available to travel to visit your accounts up to 20% of the time
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or an equivalent field of study; MS a plus but not required
  • Location: US West Required, Bay Area preferred

What you need to know about the Los Angeles Tech Scene

Los Angeles is a global leader in entertainment, so it’s no surprise that many of the biggest players in streaming, digital media and game development call the city home. But the city boasts plenty of non-entertainment innovation as well, with tech companies spanning verticals like AI, fintech, e-commerce and biotech. With major universities like Caltech, UCLA, USC and the nearby UC Irvine, the city has a steady supply of top-flight tech and engineering talent — not counting the graduates flocking to Los Angeles from across the world to enjoy its beaches, culture and year-round temperate climate.

Key Facts About Los Angeles Tech

  • Number of Tech Workers: 375,800; 5.5% of overall workforce (2024 CompTIA survey)
  • Major Tech Employers: Snap, Netflix, SpaceX, Disney, Google
  • Key Industries: Artificial intelligence, adtech, media, software, game development
  • Funding Landscape: $11.6 billion in venture capital funding in 2024 (Pitchbook)
  • Notable Investors: Strong Ventures, Fifth Wall, Upfront Ventures, Mucker Capital, Kittyhawk Ventures
  • Research Centers and Universities: California Institute of Technology, UCLA, University of Southern California, UC Irvine, Pepperdine, California Institute for Immunology and Immunotherapy, Center for Quantum Science and Engineering
