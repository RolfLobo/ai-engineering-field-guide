The Group

Morningstar DBRS provides independent credit ratings, research, and risk insights to market participants around the world. As AI becomes a practical tool for research, analysis, and operational workflows, we're building the foundations that allow teams across the business to use it responsibly and effectively.

This team sits at the intersection of software engineering, AI, and business transformation. We build the platforms, services, and tools that help Morningstar DBRS move from experimentation to real-world adoption.

The Role

We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to help shape how AI is used across Morningstar DBRS.

This is not a research role and it is not focused on building isolated proofs of concept. You'll develop the shared infrastructure, services, and applications that allow teams across the organization to deploy AI capabilities into production. You'll work closely with engineers, product partners, and business stakeholders to identify opportunities where AI can improve workflows, reduce manual effort, and support better decision-making.

You'll have significant influence over technical direction while remaining hands-on in the design, development, and operation of the systems you build.

What you'll be doing

• Design and build production AI applications using AWS Bedrock and related AWS services, with a focus on reliability, security, and maintainability.

• Develop AI agents, MCP servers, APIs, and shared platform capabilities that enable other teams to build and deploy AI-powered solutions.

• Partner with business and technology teams to understand complex workflows and identify opportunities where AI can deliver measurable improvements.

• Evaluate and implement approaches such as tool use, retrieval-based architectures, orchestration frameworks, and model evaluation techniques to improve application performance and accuracy.

• Modernize existing Python-based applications and integrate AI capabilities into products and workflows already used across Morningstar DBRS.

• Contribute to technical decisions, engineering standards, and delivery planning while helping guide less experienced engineers when needed.

What we're looking for

• Strong software engineering fundamentals with experience building and operating production applications in AWS.

• Professional experience developing applications and services in Python.

• Experience delivering AI-powered applications beyond experimentation, including deploying solutions used by internal or external customers.

• Hands-on experience with AWS Bedrock or comparable enterprise AI platforms and the surrounding ecosystem of model integration, evaluation, and monitoring.

• Experience designing and consuming APIs, building distributed services, and integrating with enterprise systems.

• A practical approach to software architecture, testing, observability, and operational support.

• Ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders to translate business problems into software solutions.

• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

What We Do

We are a global investment research and financial data company with 40-plus offices across North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia. Our products and services are used daily by individual investors, financial advisors, asset managers, retirement plan providers, and institutional investors. We provide data, research, and analysis across managed investment products, publicly listed companies, private capital markets, debt securities, and real-time global market data. The financial system can have real barriers—hidden information, friction that can slow decisions, and forces that can limit transparency and access. We work to remove them, bringing independent research, connected data, and investor-first tools to a system that needs more clarity. The people doing this work span research, technology, design, product, sales, and functional areas. We build many of our products in-house, so the work can connect directly to the tools investors use to make real financial decisions.

Why Work With Us

Imagining big things is in our blood -- it's transformed us from a company with just a few employees in 1984 to a leading independent investment research company with a worldwide presence today. As of April 2020, we acquired Sustainalytics to drive long-term meaningful outcomes for investors in the ESG space. Join us on this exciting journey!
