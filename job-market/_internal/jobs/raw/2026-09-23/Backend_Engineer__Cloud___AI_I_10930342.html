Job Description

About the Role

We are looking for a Backend Engineer, Cloud & AI Integration, to build and maintain backend services, APIs, and integrations that support AI product and internal tools.

This role will focus on Python-based backend services, REST APIs, data models, third-party integrations, and cloud-native workflows on AWS. The engineer will work closely with Full Stack Engineers to provide reliable backend services that can support scalable product development.

This is a mid-level backend engineering role suited for someone with strong hands-on experience in backend development, cloud services, APIs, and production systems. AI and Human-in-the-Loop workflows will be taught as part of the role, so prior AI or ML experience is not mandatory.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain backend services and REST APIs using Python.
  • Build and manage integrations with third-party platforms and internal systems.
  • Support AI-powered features and Human-in-the-Loop workflows where AI output is reviewed by a person before the next step.
  • Design reliable data models, backend workflows, and business logic.
  • Build serverless and cloud-native backend applications on AWS.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve production issues across backend services.
  • Collaborate with Full Stack Engineers to deliver end-to-end product features.
  • Own the backend development scope for assigned features and integrations.
  • Contribute to code reviews, testing, documentation, and release activities.
  • Ensure backend services are reliable, scalable, well-tested, and maintainable

Looking For

  • 5+ years of total experience with 3+ years of experience in backend application development and production support.
  • Hands-on experience building and maintaining Python-based backend services.
  • Strong experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
  • Experience working with AWS and cloud-native application development.
  • Experience with relational and/or NoSQL databases in production environments.
  • Experience integrating third-party APIs and external services.
  • Familiarity with Git-based development workflows.
  • Ability to work in a mid-level engineering role with strong execution and collaboration.
  • Comfortable working with Full Stack Engineers, owning the backend side of feature delivery.

Mandatory Skills

  • AWS cloud services
  • Cloud-native backend application development
  • Relational and/or NoSQL databases

  • Git-based development

  • Backend service testing and documentation

B.Soft Skills

  • Ownership of assigned backend deliverables
  • Attention to code quality, reliability, and documentation
  • Ability to troubleshoot issues in a production environment

Good to Have Skills

  • Serverless application development experience
  • Experience with asynchronous processing, messaging, or event-driven architecture
  • Experience working in Agile software development teams
  • Exposure to AI-powered product features
  • Exposure to Human-in-the-Loop workflows
