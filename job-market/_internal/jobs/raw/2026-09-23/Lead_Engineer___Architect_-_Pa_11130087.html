Role Overview:

We are looking for a hands-on Lead Engineer / Architect with strong payments, transaction processing, and AI/agent engineering experience to design and build AI-driven solutions capable of executing end-to-end transactions across multiple markets.

The role will own the technical solution from requirements and architecture through agent development, payment integration, testing, and successful transaction execution.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Design and build AI agents capable of initiating, processing, and completing end-to-end payment transactions.
  • Understand the payment transaction lifecycle and translate payment requirements into agent workflows, business rules, APIs, and system integrations.
  • Analyze market-specific payment requirements and variations and design configurable, reusable solutions.
  • Define architecture for agent orchestration, transaction state management, tool/API invocation, exception handling, retries, and recovery.
  • Integrate agents with payment platforms, gateways, transaction processing systems, APIs, and enterprise services.
  • Define appropriate boundaries between AI-driven decisioning and deterministic payment/business logic.
  • Develop primarily using Python, LLMs, and appropriate agent frameworks.
  • Implement security, authorization, auditability, observability, and guardrails around agent-driven payment actions.
  • Lead end-to-end testing and demonstrate successful transaction execution for each target market.
  • Provide hands-on technical leadership, conduct design/code reviews, and mentor engineers.

Required Experience:

  • 10+ years of software engineering experience, including architecture and technical leadership.
  • Strong hands-on Python development experience.
  • Proven experience designing and building LLM/GenAI applications and AI agents.
  • Strong understanding of payments and transaction processing.
  • Experience with payment APIs, gateways, transaction processing platforms, or payment services.
  • Strong understanding of payment lifecycle concepts such as transaction initiation, authorization, processing, transaction status, exceptions, and completion.
  • Strong experience with REST APIs, microservices, distributed systems, and cloud platforms.
  • Experience designing enterprise integrations and event-driven architectures.
  • Experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP.

AI / Agent Experience:

  • Experience building AI agents and agent-based workflows.
  • Experience with agent orchestration, tool/function calling, structured outputs, and LLM integration.
  • Experience with frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, or equivalent.
  • Experience implementing AI guardrails, evaluation, observability, and human-in-the-loop controls.
  • Ability to determine where AI should be used versus deterministic rules within payment workflows.

Preferred Experience:

  • Experience across multiple payment markets or regional payment ecosystems.
  • Knowledge of payment authorization, clearing, settlement, reconciliation, fraud/risk, disputes, or exception management.
  • Experience with Kafka or other event-streaming technologies.
  • Experience designing configurable architectures supporting market-specific payment variations.

Success Criteria:

The successful candidate will be able to take a payment transaction requirement, design the agent and supporting architecture, build the solution, integrate with the required payment systems, and demonstrate a successfully completed transaction for each designated market.

This is a hands-on architecture and engineering role. The candidate must be comfortable coding, debugging, integrating payment systems, and owning the end-to-end technical solution.

What We Do

Fractal is one of the most prominent players in the Artificial Intelligence space. Fractal's mission is to power every human decision in the enterprise and brings AI, engineering, and design to help the world's most admired Fortune 500® companies.
